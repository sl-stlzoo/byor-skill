Build Your Own Radar

Answer the radar interview on one page

Start with the same software areas and questions as the guided interview. Fill in what you know, skip what doesn’t apply, then give your assistant everything at once.

Strictly client-side. The form reads files and builds exports in your browser. It never uploads answers, calls an AI service, or saves your draft when you leave. Review what you choose to share.

Start With a Spreadsheet, If You Prefer

  1. Download the blank Thoughtworks CSV template.
  2. Keep the headers and add one row per tool or practice. Use Adopt, Trial, Assess, Caution; Techniques, Platforms, Tools, Languages & Frameworks; and TRUE or FALSE for isNew. Save as UTF-8 CSV.
  3. Import it below to add editable blips, or attach it to a new AI chat with the BYOR interview start prompt.

1. Set the Context

Leave unknowns blank. Use roles rather than staff names, and leave out secrets and confidential details.

Tailor the Ring Criteria
Set Up the Radar Story

2. Review Your Software Areas

Open the areas you know. Rename an area or add your own. Blank areas stay unanswered; mark an area not applicable if you want to skip it explicitly.

3. Add Individual Blips

Optional for the AI handoff: your assistant can draft blips from your answers above. For CSV, fill in all five Thoughtworks fields for each blip. Area and next action are for the assistant only.

4. Review & Export

Copy the JSON prompt into a new chat. The CSV contains only blip rows, not your interview notes. Neither action sends anything to an assistant or publishes your radar.
