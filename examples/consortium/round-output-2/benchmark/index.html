How to read this, and the privacy rules

  • Adopt with under 6 months of real use is counted as Trial.
  • Trial with no real use is counted as Assess.
  • Tools used by fewer than the minimum cohort of organisations are not shown.
  • Anonymous ratings are included in a tool's totals only when at least the minimum cohort of anonymous organisations used it; otherwise totals cover named organisations only.

Rings follow the canonical radar idea: Adopt is proven here, Trial needs real use, Assess is being explored, Caution reflects a negative experience. Each organisation also reported how strict its own rings are; see the calibration data in the download. Anonymous contributors are never named. Named organisations agreed to be shown against their ratings. This is an informal benchmark of opinions, not a vendor ranking, audit or endorsement.
